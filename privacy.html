Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

What the app accesses

EquipMaster Backup requests only the Google Drive scope drive.file. With this scope it can create, read, update and delete only the files and folders that it has created itself in the signed-in user's Google Drive. It cannot see any other files in that Drive.

What data is stored

The app uploads backup archives (.zip files and their small description files) produced by the owner's internal EquipMaster server into a folder named EquipMasterBackups in the owner's own Google Drive, and deletes archives older than 30 days from that folder. No other Google user data is collected.

Sharing

Data accessed through Google APIs is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising and not transferred anywhere other than between the owner's private server and the owner's own Google Drive.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
